About this loan

What is a home loan?

A home loan helps you finance the purchase or construction of a home and repay it in monthly instalments over a chosen tenure.

Fincrest 360 helps you understand your options, estimate your EMI and continue with the relevant lending institution.

Eligibility

  • Minimum age as per the lender
  • Stable source of income
  • Acceptable credit history as per the lender
  • Property that meets the lender's criteria

Indicative only. Final eligibility is decided by the lending institution.

Documents commonly required

  • Identity proof: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport or Driving Licence
  • Address proof
  • Income proof: salary slips, ITR or bank statements
  • Property documents: sale agreement, title deed and related records

The exact list may vary by lending institution.

FAQs

Common questions

What is a home loan?

A home loan is financing that lets you buy, build or renovate a home and repay the amount in monthly instalments.

Can I use a home loan to build a house?

Many lenders offer finance for construction on land you own. Eligibility and conditions are set by the lending institution.

Who decides the interest rate and tenure?

The lending institution decides these based on its own policies and your profile.
