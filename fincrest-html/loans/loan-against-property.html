About this loan

What is a loan against property?

A loan against property (LAP) is a secured loan where an eligible residential or commercial property is offered as security to the lender.

Fincrest 360 helps you understand how it works, plan your repayments and continue with the relevant lending institution.

Eligibility

  • Ownership of an eligible property with clear title
  • Stable repayment capacity
  • Acceptable credit history as per the lender
  • Property that meets the lender's valuation criteria

Indicative only. Final eligibility is decided by the lending institution.

Documents commonly required

  • Identity proof: PAN, Aadhaar or equivalent
  • Address proof
  • Income documents: salary slips, ITR or bank statements
  • Property title deed and ownership records
  • Documents requested by the lender for valuation

The exact list may vary by lending institution.

FAQs

Common questions

What is a loan against property?

It is a loan taken by pledging an eligible property as security. The property stays with you while the loan is being repaid, subject to the lender's terms.

Which properties may be considered?

Lenders typically consider eligible residential or commercial properties. The exact criteria are set by the lending institution.

Who values the property?

The lending institution carries out valuation and legal checks as part of its process.
