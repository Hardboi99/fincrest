FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Need a human answer? Contact us.

What is an EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment: the fixed amount you pay every month towards your loan, covering part of the principal and the interest.

What is the difference between secured and unsecured loans?

A secured loan, such as a home loan, car loan, gold loan or loan against property, is backed by an asset. An unsecured loan, such as a personal loan, does not need you to pledge an asset.

Who decides whether my loan is approved?

The relevant lending institution decides approval, interest rate, tenure and charges based on its own policies and your profile.

What does Fincrest 360 do?

We help you explore loan options, understand the process and continue with the relevant lending institution.

How can I check my EMI before applying?

Use the EMI calculator. It uses the standard reducing-balance formula to show an illustrative EMI, total interest and total repayment.

What documents do I usually need?

Identity proof, address proof, income proof or bank statements and photographs are commonly requested. See the checklist below.

Does a longer tenure always cost less?

A longer tenure lowers each EMI but usually increases the total interest paid over the loan.

How do I become a DSA?

Visit the DSA page and fill in the enquiry form. Our team will get in touch with the next steps.

Document checklist

Keep these ready

Commonly requested documents. The exact list depends on the loan type and the lending institution.

Identity proof

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport
  • Driving Licence
  • Voter ID

Address proof

  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport
  • Utility bill
  • Rent agreement
  • Bank statement with address

Income proof

  • Recent salary slips
  • Bank statements
  • Income Tax Returns (ITR)
  • Form 16
  • Business financial statements

Other documents

  • Passport-size photographs
  • Property documents (secured loans)
  • Dealer quotation (vehicle loans)
  • Gold ornaments for valuation (gold loan)
