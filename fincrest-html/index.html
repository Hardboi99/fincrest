FAQs

Common questions.

Clear answers before you calculate or continue with a lending institution.

More questions
What loan solutions can I explore with Fincrest 360?

You can explore home loans, loan against property, car loans, bike loans, gold loans, personal loans and business loans.

How does the EMI calculator work?

It uses the standard reducing-balance formula with the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure you enter, and shows your estimated monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment.

Who determines my final loan interest rate?

The relevant lending institution decides the interest rate, tenure and charges based on its own policies and your profile.

Does Fincrest 360 directly approve loans?

No. Approval decisions rest with the lending institution. Fincrest 360 helps you understand your options and guides you through the enquiry.

What documents may be required?

Lenders commonly ask for identity proof, address proof, income proof or bank statements and photographs. The exact list depends on the loan type and the lending institution.

How do I submit a loan enquiry?

Fill in the enquiry form with your requirement. Our team will get in touch with you with the next steps.

Can I compare different repayment tenures?

Yes. The calculator shows how the EMI and total interest change for different tenures. Which tenures are available depends on the lending institution and the loan product.

Are the EMI calculator results guaranteed?

No. They are illustrative and based on the inputs you provide. Actual terms, rates, charges and approval are determined by the lending institution.
